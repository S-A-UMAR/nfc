Creator Responsibility: When you publish photos, company logos, biographical text, and links on your {{ BRAND_NAME }} profile, you represent and warrant that you own or possess legal rights to that material. You are solely responsible for all content published through your account.

01. Scope of User-Generated Content

User-Generated Content ("UGC") encompasses all data, media, and text you provide or upload to the platform, including:

  • Profile and avatar photographs uploaded to Cloudinary;
  • Full name, display name, job titles, company names, and professional bios;
  • Custom links, external social URLs, portfolio links, and WhatsApp routing numbers;
  • Services, product catalog titles, descriptions, and imagery created within our platform;
  • Physical contact addresses and location descriptions.

02. Content Standards & Warranties

By publishing any UGC on {{ BRAND_NAME }}, you expressly represent and warrant that:

  • Legal Ownership & Authorization: You own the copyright to the uploaded photographs and text, or you have obtained valid written licenses from the copyright or trademark owners.
  • Truthful Identity: Your biographical information, qualifications, employment claims, and corporate affiliations are accurate, truthful, and not deceptive.
  • No Infringement: The content does not violate any third party's trademark, trade secret, privacy rights, publicity rights, or intellectual property rights.
  • Compliance with Law: The content complies with all applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and the jurisdiction in which you operate.

03. Prohibited Content Categories

The following categories of UGC are strictly prohibited and will be removed immediately upon detection:

  • Impersonation & Phishing: Profile photos or logos designed to mimic banks (e.g. GTBank, Zenith, Access), payment providers, regulatory agencies (e.g. CBN, EFCC, NAFDAC), or other individuals without documented authorization.
  • False Professional Claims: Profiles deceptively claiming licensed professional status (e.g. Medical Doctor, Barrister/Legal Practitioner, Chartered Accountant, Licensed Aviation Pilot) without legitimate credentials.
  • Explicit or Obscene Imagery: Nudity, pornography, sexually suggestive content, or graphic depictions of violence.
  • Defamation & Malicious Attacks: Uploading content specifically designed to ruin an individual's reputation, dox private home addresses, or blackmail others.
  • Illegal Goods & Services: Promoting unregistered pharmaceuticals, prohibited weapons, illicit narcotics, or fraudulent investment opportunities.

04. Moderation & Platform Rights

While {{ BRAND_NAME }} does not review every uploaded image prior to publishing, we maintain active administrative rights:

  • Right to Inspect: We reserve the right (but assume no ongoing obligation) to review, monitor, and investigate UGC published on public URLs.
  • Right to Remove or Edit: We reserve the right to remove, blur, edit, or restrict any UGC that, in our sole discretion, violates this policy, our Acceptable Use Policy, or applicable law.
  • Hardware Suspension: Where an offending profile is tied to a physical NFC card, we will immediately disable cloud redirection for that hardware card.

05. How to Report Offensive or Infringing Content

If you encounter an {{ BRAND_NAME }} profile displaying inappropriate, fraudulent, or infringing UGC, please submit a report with the profile link to our Content Moderation & Abuse Desk or email {{ BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. We review all verified complaints within twenty-four (24) business hours.
