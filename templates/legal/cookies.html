01. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone by websites you visit. They are widely used to make web applications function efficiently, preserve authenticated user sessions, and provide essential security protections.

03. Non-Essential & Third-Party Trackers (None Deployed)

Many web services load invasive third-party trackers. At {{ BRAND_NAME }}, we maintain strict technical boundaries:

  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy Google AdSense, Meta/Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or ad retargeting cookies.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Cookies: Our profile metrics (taps, link clicks, views) are logged server-side as first-party telemetry. We do not inject third-party analytics scripts that track you across other websites.
  • No Selling of Visitor Profiles: We do not package or sell cookie data to third-party data brokers.

04. Managing & Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

Because the cookies we set are strictly necessary for authentication and form security, disabling them in your browser will prevent you from logging into your customer dashboard or completing orders. However, if you wish to inspect or block cookies, you can do so through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari: Settings / Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
