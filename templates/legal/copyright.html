03. Notice & Takedown Procedure (Copyright Infringement)

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe that content published on any {{ BRAND_NAME }} public profile (/u/<slug>/) infringes upon your copyright under the Nigerian Copyright Act 2022 or international law, you may submit a formal Infringement Notice containing:

  1. Identification of the Work: A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed, including registration details or online proof of ownership.
  2. Identification of Infringing Content: The exact URL (e.g. https://uzyra.onrender.com/u/example-slug/) and specific image or text claimed to be infringing.
  3. Contact Information: Your full legal name, company name (if applicable), email address, physical address, and telephone number.
  4. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you hold a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Accuracy Statement & Signature: A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner, accompanied by an electronic or physical signature.
