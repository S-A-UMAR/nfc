Nationwide Nigeria Delivery: {{ BRAND_NAME }} ships physical smart cards to all thirty-six (36) states of Nigeria and the Federal Capital Territory (Abuja). Deliveries are coordinated via trusted commercial dispatch and courier logistics partners.

01. Geographic Coverage

We currently fulfill and deliver physical smart card orders nationwide within Nigeria. International shipments outside Nigeria can be arranged on a bespoke basis by contacting our corporate enterprise team.

02. Order Processing & Fulfillment Timelines

Each physical card order moves through two distinct stages:

Stage 1: Production & Programming (1–3 Business Days)

Every individual card is individually encoded with a secure, unique chip identifier, linked to your cloud redirection profile, electronically tested for RF induction, and packed in luxury protective packaging.

Stage 2: Courier Transit Timelines

Destination Region Estimated Courier Transit Delivery Method
Lagos State 1 – 3 Business Days Express Local Courier / Direct Dispatch
Abuja (FCT) 2 – 4 Business Days Domestic Air Logistics / Inter-state Courier
Major Commercial Hubs (PH, Kano, Ibadan, Enugu, Benin) 3 – 5 Business Days Inter-state Interstate Freight Logistics
Other State Capitals & Regional Towns 4 – 8 Business Days Regional Courier Terminal / Doorstep Dispatch

Note: Business days exclude weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) and statutory Nigerian public holidays. Unforeseen weather, transit checkpoints, or national fuel constraints may occasionally introduce slight delays.

03. Customer Address & Contact Requirements

To ensure prompt and successful doorstep delivery, you must provide:

  • Complete Street Address: Street name, house/building number, landmark or estate name, local government area (LGA), and state. Vague descriptions or P.O. Box addresses cannot be accepted.
  • Active Telephone Number: A working Nigerian phone number that can receive phone calls from dispatch riders on the delivery day. Couriers will call before attempting doorstep drop-off.
  • Authorized Receiver: If you will not be present at the delivery location, ensure a colleague, security personnel, or family member is authorized to inspect and sign for the package.

04. Failed Deliveries & Redelivery Attempts

Courier partners will attempt delivery up to two (2) times. If delivery fails because the recipient is repeatedly unreachable, the phone number is switched off, or the address is incorrect:

  • The package will be held at the local courier distribution hub for forty-eight (48) hours.
  • Our operations team will contact you via email or WhatsApp to coordinate updated contact instructions.
  • If a package is returned to our central operations center due to unresponsiveness or inaccurate address, a re-dispatch fee will be charged to reship the package.

05. Lost or Damaged Shipments

  • Damaged Package: If the packaging arrives visibly crushed or torn, please take a photo before opening and inspect the card. Report any damage within seven (7) days under our Refund Policy.
  • Lost in Transit: If your order exceeds the estimated transit window by more than seven (7) business days and tracking confirms the parcel is lost, {{ BRAND_NAME }} will manufacture and dispatch a free replacement card at zero cost to you.

06. Delivery Inquiries & Order Tracking

You can check your order fulfillment status at any time through your Customer Orders Dashboard. For urgent delivery inquiries or address changes before dispatch, reach out to our logistics desk at {{ BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL }} or via WhatsApp at {{ BRAND_SUPPORT_PHONE }}.
