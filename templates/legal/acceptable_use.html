01. Purpose & Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to any individual or business registering an account, publishing a digital profile, programming an NFC card, or utilizing {{ BRAND_NAME }} web services. By accessing our services, you agree to comply with this policy in full.

02. Strictly Prohibited Activities

You may not use {{ BRAND_NAME }} services, profiles, or physical hardware cards to engage in, facilitate, or promote any of the following activities:

A. Fraud, Deception & Impersonation

  • Advance-Fee & Investment Fraud: Operating or promoting Ponzi schemes, multi-level marketing scams, fake investment clubs, cryptocurrency doubling schemes, or advance-fee fraud (commonly termed "419").
  • Impersonation: Falsely claiming to be an employee, agent, executive, or representative of a bank, financial institution, government agency (e.g. CBN, EFCC, FIRS, CAC), or existing legitimate company without authorization.
  • Counterfeit Credentials: Displaying fabricated academic degrees, fake professional certifications (e.g. medical, legal, accounting, engineering), or bogus corporate registration credentials.

B. Cyber Threats, Malware & Security Exploits

  • Phishing & Credential Harvesting: Creating digital profiles or custom button links that deceive visitors into entering passwords, banking OTPs, or financial credentials.
  • Malicious Payloads: Linking to malware, ransomware, spyware, trojans, or exploit kits.
  • NFC Microchip Tampering: Reprogramming physical {{ BRAND_NAME }} cards to transmit malicious NDEF payloads designed to exploit vulnerabilities in reading devices.
  • System Abuse: Deploying automated scripts, scrapers, credential-stuffing tools, or denial-of-service (DDoS) traffic against {{ BRAND_NAME }} servers.

C. Harassment, Violence & Illegal Content

  • Defamation & Harassment: Publishing content that slanders, bullies, blackmails, stalks, or threatens individuals or groups.
  • Hate Speech & Extremism: Content promoting violence, discrimination, or hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, or sexual orientation.
  • Explicit Adult Content: Publishing non-consensual imagery, prostitution listings, pornography, or exploitative material.
  • Unlawful Goods: Advertising illegal drugs, unregistered firearms, stolen property, or regulated substances without proper licenses.

D. Intellectual Property Violations

  • Unauthorized use of registered trademarks, business names, or copyright-protected logos to create a misleading appearance of official corporate affiliation.
  • Uploading copyrighted photography, art, or written material without explicit permission or legal right.

03. Monitoring, Investigation & Enforcement

{{ BRAND_NAME }} actively protects its user community and the integrity of smart networking in Nigeria:

  • Proactive Review & Anomaly Detection: We employ automated heuristics and administrative audits to detect abusive profiles, unusual card tap spikes, and malicious redirect links.
  • Investigation: Upon receiving an abuse notice or detecting suspicious behavior, we reserve the right to review the user's published profile content and outbound links.
  • Immediate Sanctions: In the event of an AUP breach, we may without prior notice:
    1. Deactivate the user's public profile URL immediately.
    2. Suspend and permanently blacklist associated physical NFC cards from redirecting.
    3. Terminate the user account and retain records for law enforcement reporting.
    4. Report illegal activities to appropriate statutory bodies (such as the Nigeria Police Force or EFCC).

04. Reporting Abuse or Impersonation

If you encounter an {{ BRAND_NAME }} profile or smart card that violates this policy—or if someone is impersonating you or your business—please report it immediately through our Abuse & Impersonation Reporting Desk or email {{ BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with the offending profile URL.
