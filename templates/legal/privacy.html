  • Account Registration: First name, last name, email address, password hash, unique referral code, and optional telephone number.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you access our platform, public profile endpoints, or tap an NFC card redirect, our infrastructure records standard technical request logs:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) Address: Used strictly for rate-limiting, authentication security, fraud detection, and preventing automated abuse.
  • Device & Browser Data: Browser User-Agent header (browser family, version, operating system) truncated and stored for responsive layout optimization.
  • Referral Information: HTTP Referer URL indicating how you arrived at a profile or public page.
  • Timestamps: Exact date and time of user actions, login sessions, and card interactions.

C. Payment Data Handled by Paystack

All financial payment transactions for physical NFC cards and services are handled directly by Paystack Payments Limited, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment service provider regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). {{ BRAND_NAME }} never collects, processes, or stores your payment card numbers, CVV security codes, or bank account PINs on our servers. We receive only a cryptographic transaction reference token, transaction status, and amount paid to confirm your order.

  • Account Deletion: Upon a verified account deletion request, your public profile (/u/<slug>/) is immediately taken offline, associated physical cards are unlinked and deactivated, and personal profile information is removed from active production access.

07. Your Rights Under the NDPA 2023

As a data subject in Nigeria or visiting user, you hold recognized statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data {{ BRAND_NAME }} holds concerning you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can correct, update, or edit your personal profile information directly through your customer dashboard at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your account and personal data, subject to legitimate legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we suspend active processing of your data while a dispute or objection is investigated.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your core profile information in a structured, commonly used digital format (e.g., standard .vcf contact card).
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) (ndpc.gov.ng) if you believe your data protection rights have been infringed.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a formal request via our Complaints & Data Rights Desk or email {{ BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. We respond to verified data subject requests within 30 calendar days as required by law.

08. Technical & Operational Security

{{ BRAND_NAME }} implements robust technical, organizational, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized disclosure, accidental destruction, alteration, or unlawful access:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All data transmitted between your device and {{ BRAND_NAME }} is encrypted using modern TLS cryptographic protocols with HSTS enforcement.
  • Cryptographic Password Protection: Account passwords are never stored in plain text; they are hashed using secure cryptographic stretching algorithms (PBKDF2 with SHA-256).
  • Hardened Session Cookies: Cookies are marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and CSRF interception.
  • NFC Hardware Separation: Physical NFC cards do not store account passwords, contact databases, or financial numbers. They contain only a public redirect web link.
