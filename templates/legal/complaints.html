Formal Dispute Escalation: Every inquiry or complaint submitted through this desk is logged with a timestamp and reviewed directly by our compliance and operations team. We acknowledge all formal submissions within twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) business hours.

01. Categories of Inquiries Handled

You can use this desk to submit formal notices and complaints across any of the following areas:

  • Data Subject Privacy Requests (NDPA 2023): Requests to access, correct, export, or permanently delete personal data held by {{ BRAND_NAME }}.
  • IP & Copyright Infringement Notices: Formal notifications regarding unauthorized use of copyrighted photos, trademarks, or brand assets on public profiles.
  • Fraud & Impersonation Reports: Urgent notifications regarding deceptive profiles mimicking banks, government officials, or corporate brands.
  • NFC Hardware & Delivery Disputes: Non-functional NFC chips, transit damages, or courier delivery delays across Nigeria.
  • Billing & Payment Inquiries: Payment verification, duplicate charge investigations, and refund requests processed via Paystack.
  • General Platform Feedback: Technical bugs, service grievances, or general platform inquiries.

02. Resolution Procedure & Timelines

STEP 1: RECEIPT
24 – 48 Hours

Automated logging, assignment to a compliance officer, and formal email acknowledgment.

STEP 2: INVESTIGATION
3 – 5 Business Days

Technical log review, courier reconciliation, or content audit with internal operations.

STEP 3: RESOLUTION
Within 10 Days

Final binding resolution, replacement card dispatch, content removal, or NDPA compliance notice.
