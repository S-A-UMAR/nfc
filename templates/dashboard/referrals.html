How the Referral Program Works

  • Qualification Rule: A referral qualifies when the referred user registers a new UZYRA account via your link, verifies their email, and completes one paid physical NFC card order verified by Paystack.
  • Reward Value: ₦2,000 in UZYRA purchase credits per qualified purchase.
  • Fraud & Anti-Self Referral: Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and cancelled/refunded orders are automatically rejected to protect platform integrity.
  • Privacy Notice: Referred user identities are masked in public displays to protect personal data in compliance with NDPA 2023.
