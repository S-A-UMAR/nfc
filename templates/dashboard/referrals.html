How the Referral Programme Works

  • Milestone 1: Refer 10 genuine, unique new users who register and verify their email address via your link.
  • Milestone 2: At least 1 of those referred users must complete a successful paid STRIDE card order and activate their physical card.
  • Reward: One free STRIDE Smart NFC Card + free nationwide delivery. Non-transferable, non-cash.
  • Upgrade: You may upgrade to a higher package by paying the price difference.
  • Fraud Prevention: Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and unverified or refunded orders are automatically excluded.
  • Privacy: Referred user identities are masked in this display (NDPA 2023 compliance).
  • Review: All reward claims are reviewed by the STRIDE team before fulfilment.
