Need help activating your card?
Where is my Activation PIN?

The 8-character security PIN is printed on the protective card sleeve or inside the physical packaging box sent with your card.

NFC tap didn't trigger automatically?

Make sure NFC is turned on in your phone's settings (Settings → Connected Devices / NFC on Android; iPhone XR and newer read NFC automatically at the top-rear edge). Alternatively, scan the QR code on the back of the card.
